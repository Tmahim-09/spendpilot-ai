# SpendPilot AI: Hackathon Video Demonstration Script

**Target Duration**: ~2 minutes and 45 seconds  
**Tone**: Confident, fast-paced, product-focused, professional  
**Audio**: English narration or clear English voiceover with visual captions  

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] Hook: The Online Shopping Problem & Introduction
**Visual**: Open on a traditional e-commerce page with 20 confusing filter dropdowns and endless product grids. Cut to the clean, modern SpendPilot AI interface.  
**Narrator**:
> *"Finding the right product online today is tedious. You're forced to navigate endless filter menus, compare confusing spec sheets across dozen tabs, and deal with AI chatbots that hallucinate fake specs and outdated prices.*  
> *Meet SpendPilot AI — your personal shopping assistant powered by Google Gemini, grounded in real-time retail data from Channel3, and equipped with PayPal multi-seller checkout."*

---

### [0:20 - 0:55] Conversational Need Analysis & Intelligent Delegation
**Visual**: Screen recording of user typing in SpendPilot chat:  
`"I need a laptop for professional web development under $1,200"`  
SpendPilot responds asking for Operating System preferences with quick-reply buttons.  
User types: `"u suggest"`  
The bouncing thinking indicator appears (`SpendPilot is thinking...`), followed by SpendPilot warmly explaining it will recommend top all-around options for web development, and immediately starts retrieving verified laptops.  
**Narrator**:
> *"Instead of filling out rigid forms, you talk to SpendPilot naturally. Here, we ask for a web development laptop under $1,200. Notice how SpendPilot understands the goal, extracts the budget limit, and clarifies key requirements.*  
> *When we delegate with 'u suggest', SpendPilot doesn't get confused or re-ask questions. It explains its recommendation strategy and queries live merchant catalogs instantly."*

---

### [0:55 - 1:25] Grounded Channel3 Discovery & Spec Comparison
**Visual**: Channel3 product cards appear with real prices, merchant badges (e.g. Best Buy, B&H), and verified specs. User clicks "Compare" on three laptop options. The side-by-side comparison modal opens showing detailed spec rows: CPU, RAM, Storage, Battery Life, and Price.  
**Narrator**:
> *"These aren't hallucinated products. Through Channel3, SpendPilot retrieves verified merchant offers with live pricing and stock availability.*  
> *With one click, our AI comparison engine standardizes conflicting spec sheets into a clear, side-by-side matrix, highlighting critical trade-offs so you can decide with total confidence."*

---

### [1:25 - 1:50] Persistent Multi-Task Shopping Workspace
**Visual**: User selects the MacBook Air from the comparison view. It smoothly animates into "My Shopping List" on the right.  
User then types: `"I also need noise-cancelling headphones under $200"`.  
SpendPilot switches tasks, retrieves headphones within budget, and adds a pair to the cart.  
**Narrator**:
> *"Once selected, the item is saved to your persistent shopping workspace. Notice how you can seamlessly add another item — like headphones under $200. SpendPilot isolates the new search without leaking earlier laptop specs into the headphone query."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:35] PayPal Multi-Seller Sandbox Checkout
**Visual**: Navigate to the Cart / PayPal Demo page. The cart displays two items from distinct demo merchants:  
- *SpendPilot Audio Lab* ($149.00)  
- *SpendPilot Workspace Gear* ($89.00)  
User clicks the official yellow **PayPal Checkout** button.  
PayPal Sandbox popup opens. User logs in with Sandbox buyer credentials and confirms payment.  
Popup closes. The verified receipt screen appears, highlighting the distinct Capture IDs for Seller A and Seller B.  
**Narrator**:
> *"Now for the payment innovation. Often, your ideal setup comes from multiple distinct sellers. Normally, that means multiple separate transactions.*  
> *With SpendPilot's PayPal Orders v2 integration, we construct a server-authoritative multi-seller order with separate purchase units.*  
> *The buyer approves once through PayPal's secure modal. On the backend, SpendPilot executes split captures atomically. Both sellers receive their allocated funds with independent transaction receipts."*

---

### [2:35 - 2:50] Summary & Closing
**Visual**: Cut back to the SpendPilot homepage with the verified receipt badge and technology logos (Gemini, Channel3, PayPal, Next.js).  
**Narrator**:
> *"SpendPilot AI transforms e-commerce from high-friction searching into effortless conversational discovery, backed by real retail data and seamless PayPal multi-seller checkout.*  
> *Thank you, judges!"*
